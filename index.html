Contract Controls

Key controls are disclosed for transparency without exposing unnecessary technical detail.

Administrative Controls: The contract owner can pause or resume transfers and adjust the sell tax up to the hard-coded 10% maximum. The owner can also update the designated tax wallet and manage tax-exempt addresses.

These controls are disclosed for transparency; they do not mean the owner can create unlimited KHAN supply or arbitrarily withdraw holders’ KHAN.

The contract does not provide an owner function for unlimited minting, arbitrary holder blacklisting, router replacement, pair replacement, or arbitrary withdrawal of the contract's KHAN tokens.

Current transaction taxes: Buy 0% · Sell 5% · Transfer 0%. Sell tax maximum: 10%.

Ownership Renouncement: The team intends to renounce ownership after liquidity has been locked and trading has remained stable for a reasonable period, at the team's discretion. The exact timing will depend on market conditions and will be announced publicly to the community when it happens.
