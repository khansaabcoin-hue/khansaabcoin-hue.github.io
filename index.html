Frequently Asked Questions

‎

Tap a question to expand.

‎ ‎
What is KHAN SAAB Coin? ‎
‎KHAN SAAB Coin is a community-driven digital token focused on transparency, fair distribution, and public participation. The goal is to build a strong community before the official token launch. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
What is the Community Support Phase? ‎
‎This is the early participation stage where supporters can join before the official token launch and help build the foundation of the project. Allocations are recorded transparently. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
How does Early Allocation work? ‎
‎Supporters provide their wallet address and participate during this phase. Their allocation is recorded securely and tokens will be transferred with verifiable proof during distribution. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
What is the token price? ‎
‎During the early phase, the entry rate is 1 PKR = 1 KHAN Coin. This is not the market price and may change after listing. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
Where will the token be launched? ‎
‎The token is planned to be launched on PancakeSwap after completion of the community phase and liquidity setup. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
How will I verify my tokens? ‎
‎Each transfer will include a transaction hash which can be verified publicly on BscScan. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
Is there a maximum limit per wallet? ‎
‎Yes, limits may be applied to ensure fair distribution among all participants. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
Will updates be shared publicly? ‎
‎Yes, all major updates including allocation records, distribution, and launch steps will be shared with the community for transparency. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
What makes this project different? ‎
‎KHAN SAAB Coin focuses on a public-first approach with simple entry, visible allocation, and community-driven growth instead of hype-based promotion. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
Is this a scam? ‎
‎No. The project is being built with a transparency-first approach. All allocations will be recorded and shared publicly. No hidden supply will exist. Community updates will be continuous. However, participants should always do their own research before joining. ‎
‎
‎ ‎
What is the founder's vision? ‎
‎The vision is to create a simple, transparent, and community-first token where ordinary people can participate early without complex barriers. The focus is on building trust and long-term growth instead of short-term hype. ‎
